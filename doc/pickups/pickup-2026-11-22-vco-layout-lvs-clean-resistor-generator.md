### efx poly resistor generators fixed (glayout_test, then copied to `mag/efx.tcl`)

- **Length parameter.** `nl` became `rl`, the extracted (schematic) length in L: l = rl·L, rl ≥ 3.
  - Like the PDK's generator (`l_delta −0.08` in `sky130A.tcl`), the body is drawn rl·L − 0.16, because `sky130A.tech` extracts the body + 0.16 (`l=l+0.16`).
  - The cell stays (rl + 24)·L tall with the head li on the L grid. Only the poly ends and the head/body edges are 0.08 off the grid.
- **New `nwell` option.** It paints an n-well under the resistor (L past the li, ≥ 0.84 wide) with a `B` port on it, so the body extracts as the well even cell by cell.
- **Cell names** are now `ppolyres_w<w>_rl<rl>[_nwell]`. Old `…_l<nl>` cells keep their geometry (extracting nl·L + 0.16). The dialog reads an old cell's `nl` as `rl`.
- **Verified:**
  - 47/47 cells pass `compare_efx.py`, including the new `rp_nwell` and `rx_nwell`;
  - `verify.sh` on all 12 resistor cells: l = rl·L everywhere.
  - **Expected DRC flags only:**
    - `m_rp_min` and `m_rx_min` fail rpm.1a/urpm.1a (a lone resistor narrower than 0.87);
    - the n-well cells give Magic nwell.4 (no tap in a lone cell), and their `m_` versions also fail rpm.1a/urpm.1a (lone w 0.69).
